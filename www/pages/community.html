01 — research

Methods from papers, in OCaml.

sofo-raven

hennequin-lab

SOFO, a second-order optimizer that never backpropagates. Each step measures the loss and its curvature in a random subspace of the parameters with one batched forward-mode pass, so its memory does not grow with the length of a sequence.

Yu, Xia, Ma, Lengyel and Hennequin, NeurIPS 2024 ↗

code ↗

symo-raven

hennequin-lab

SYMO estimates a network's curvature from gradients averaged over the symmetries of its weights, and preconditions gradient descent with the estimate.

Artemev, Xia, Boyd, Yu, Dangel, Hennequin and Bernacchia, ICML 2026 ↗

02 — libraries

Libraries on top of Raven.

ModelKit

asara-io

Classical machine learning, like scikit-learn: immutable estimator specifications, pipelines fitted only on training rows, deterministic cross-validation and search, and portable fitted models. Its modelkit-nx and modelkit-talon packages take Nx tensors and Talon columns, checked.

code ↗

geom

hennequin-lab

Batched geometric algebra for robotics: Euclidean, projective and conformal models of space, with forward and differential kinematics. Every multivector is an Nx tensor, so it broadcasts, differentiates with Rune and compiles.

code ↗

SoundML

gabyfle

Audio processing for OCaml, on Nx: reading, writing and resampling audio files.
