roadmap / performance

Measured, losses included.

How Raven compares with the fastest stacks we know, on the workloads the road to 1.0 is judged by. Each section names its machine, the versions compared and the commit measured, and the benchmark code is in the repository.

01 — the goal post

jit-compiled gpt-oss, at least as fast as four baselines.

Beta 1's goal post, JIT compilation on GPU, is met. The next goal post on the road to 1.0: gpt-oss-20b, compiled by Rune.jit, at least as fast as tinygrad, JAX, llama.cpp and vLLM on NVIDIA (through CUDA and through NVIDIA's kernel driver directly) and on AMD. We are not there yet, and this table is where we track it.

where decode's time goes

Expert products take 5.4 ms at 212–254 GB/s, against llama.cpp's 3.0. Top-k's 20-kernel sort and about 37 small kernels a layer take about 1.5 ms; the argmax over the logits 0.63 ms.

where prefill's time goes

Expert products take 326 ms on WMMA at 7–8 TFLOPS, against 177. The out projection takes no WMMA: 52 ms, against q's 10.

Machine “nonnormal” (gfx1201) at 502431b668, the new nx and rune; attention on WMMA. First call: 1.03 s when compiling kernels, of which lowering and linking the first block take 69–131 ms on the host; llama.cpp 2.19 s without Mesa's shader cache. llama.cpp's ROCm build needs HIP libraries this machine lacks.
